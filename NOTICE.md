Source dataset: Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset (CUAD), The Atticus Project;
Hendrycks, Burns, Chen and Ball (NeurIPS 2021). Source revision and hash: `data/source.json`.

development/holdout assignment and evaluation metadata, and preserves original annotation ownership.

or claim commissioned work, expert approval or live model results that have not occurred.
