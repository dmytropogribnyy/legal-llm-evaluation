# Legal AI & LLM Evaluation

**Contract-review AI evaluation · Legal quality assurance · AI governance support**

I help teams assess whether AI-generated contract analysis is accurate, supported by the source
text and suitable for human legal review. My work combines software quality engineering with a
background in legal practice and a PhD in Business Law.

**Available for freelance and B2B engagements** with LegalTech teams, legal departments and
businesses adopting AI-assisted contract review.

## Areas of focus

- **Contract analysis quality:** identify missed provisions, unsupported quotations and inconsistent outputs.
- **LLM evaluation:** define test cases, reference criteria and reproducible comparisons for an agreed use case.
- **Legal review:** assess material errors and translate findings into practical recommendations.
- **AI governance:** support intended-use assessment, risk documentation and EU AI Act evidence mapping.

## Engagement scope

A typical proposed engagement covers an evaluation brief, an agreed test set, automated checks,
expert review and a concise findings report. Deliverables can include:

- A quality scorecard with traceable evidence and clearly stated limitations.
- A register of material errors, their business implications and recommended actions.
- Acceptance criteria and regression checks for subsequent model or prompt changes.
- An AI governance evidence-gap assessment tailored to the system's intended use.

Scope, data access and review requirements are agreed for each engagement. Governance support
does not constitute certification or an automated legal determination.

## Evaluation framework

This independently developed Python framework supports repeatable contract-review evaluation,
source-evidence checks and structured human review. Its public-data corpus covers **509 unique
contract texts across 41 CUAD categories**. Corpus coverage describes available evaluation material,
not completed model evaluations.

Published validation currently covers offline workflow checks. Model-specific findings require
actual execution and expert review. This repository presents the independently developed framework;
technical methods and validation records are linked below.
